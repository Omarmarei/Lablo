## 2. Per-app notes

### IPTV Smarters Pro and Smarters Player Lite
- **Samsung store:** The "IPTV Smarters Pro" listing is by WHMCS Smarters and was last updated 18 May 2023 ([Samsung US](https://www.samsung.com/us/appstore/app/G20264015560)). A second listing, "IPTV Smarters", was last updated October 2023 ([Samsung US](https://www.samsung.com/us/appstore/app/G18365012381)). Both say service providers "have to register" their server URL with Smarters before the app works with their panel.
  - Forum posts put that registration fee at about $120, or £90 to £300 for hosting on LG ([techkings / iptv.community threads](https://www.techkings.org/threads/iptv-smarters-player-samsung-smart-tv.147277/)) **[U, posts are years old]**.
  - So on TVs, Smarters is effectively provider-gated. An end user with an unregistered provider can't log in.
- **TV features:** Xtream Codes API, live/VOD/series, catch-up, EPG, parental control, account expiry display, multiple users.
- **Smarters Player Lite (iOS, iPadOS, tvOS, macOS, visionOS):** 4.8 from 453K ratings, $1.99 premium unlock, M3U and JSON support ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/smarters-player-lite/id1628995509)). A 2026 UK guide says it is not in the LG store and is missing from some regional Samsung stores ([getlivetv.uk, Jul 2026](https://getlivetv.uk/installation-guides/smart-tv/)) **[U]**.
- **Controversies:**
  - Removed from Google Play for the third time in October 2019 ([TorrentFreak](https://torrentfreak.com/popular-iptv-smarters-app-removed-from-google-play-following-complaint/)).
  - A Madrid court ordered Google to remove it after a LaLiga complaint in December 2022 ([TorrentFreak](https://torrentfreak.com/laliga-court-orders-google-to-wipe-iptv-smarters-pro-from-play-store-230102/)).
  - The App Store and the LG store are full of lookalike "Smarters" apps, some with weekly subscriptions ([Xataka](https://www.xatakahome.com/a-fondo/mejores-aplicaciones-iptv-para-smart-tv-lg-webos)).

### The IBO family: IBO Player, IBO Player Pro, ibo Pro Player, "IBO Cast"
- **IBO Player** (iboplayer.com):
  - 7-day trial, then €10 one-time per device, tied to the device ID ([Genova review, Oct 2026](https://www.genovatv.net/en/iptv-apps/ibo-player); vendor source).
  - Playlists can only be managed on the web portal, using the device ID and key. M3U and Xtream are supported; Stalker/MAC portals are not.
  - Has EPG, catch-up, favourites and parental lock. No recording or multiview. Each new TV costs another €10.
  - The App Store listing "IBO PLAYER" (seller ibrahim akoum) is rated 2.7 from 23 ratings, costs $9.99 to activate, and sends uploads to iboiptv.com ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ibo-player/id1547652240)).
- **IBO Player Pro:** the same developer's newer app. A 2025 press release says existing activations carry over, and that "our only official website is iboplayer.com". The App Store version (ibrahim akoum) costs $5.99 and is rated 4.2 from 5 ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1669696096)).
- **ibo Pro Player:** a different publisher, IRBOETECH CO., LIMITED (iboproapp.com).
  - App Store: 4.8 from 14K ratings, $9.99 activation ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6449647925)).
  - Platforms include Samsung, LG, Roku, Windows and Whale OS. Playlists can be managed in the app or on the website ([iboproapp.com](https://iboproapp.com/)).
- **Ibosol, a white-label network:** About 24 skins of what appears to be one product are sold as €8–10 lifetime activations. They include IBO Player, BOB Player, BOB Pro, BOB Premium, MAC Player, IBOSS, ABE, Family, FlixNet, King4K, Virginia, KTN, Duplex TV and SmartOne Pro ([store.ibosol.info](https://store.ibosol.info/); [ibosol.name](https://ibosol.name/)).
  - A reseller panel sells credits: 1 credit = 1 year, 2 credits = "lifetime (until 2052)" ([flyboxtv](https://flyboxtv.com/product/ibosol-panel/)).
  - Several storefronts each claim to be the official one ([ibosol.org](https://www.ibosol.org/mylist)). Who owns what is **unverified**.
- **Reputation:** Trustpilot gives iboproapp.com 1.6 from 87 reviews, 84% of them 1-star ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/iboproapp.com)), and iboplayer.com 2.0 from 12 ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/iboplayer.com)).
  - Common complaints: no support, refusals to refund, and licences that can't be moved to a new device. Support is quoted as saying "Switching MAC activation from one device to another is disabled, for all our Ibosol apps".
  - Privacy risk: the developer holds users' playlist credentials, and fake portals exist (Genova).
- **"IBO Cast":** I found no app by this name. It is probably a confusion with the names above **[U]**.

## 3. Market gaps and patterns

### Pricing norms
- **Trials:** 7 days is standard. Hot Player gives 30 days, SmartOne 15, Easy IP TV 24 hours.
- **After the trial:** usually €5–13 one-time per device, or €2.50–8 a year. SIPTV €5.49, IBO €10, SmartOne €12.99, Net €6.79, Hot €19.99 lifetime.
- **Devices:** almost always one MAC per licence, non-transferable, non-refundable. The exceptions are Smart STB Full (5 devices, defined transfer rules), TiviMate (5 devices) and StreamHub (3 TVs).
- **Resellers:** credit panels are normal (Ibosol: 1 credit = 1 year, 2 = lifetime). IPTV sellers bundle app activations.
- **Smarters:** charges providers rather than users, which locks TV users into registered providers.

### What nearly all of them do badly
1. **Onboarding.** You read a MAC or device key off the TV, go to a website on another device, paste a URL, then restart the app. MACs change between Wi-Fi and Ethernet (SET). Few apps let you type the playlist on the TV; SIPTV, IBO and Duplex make you use the portal. I found no verified QR-code or phone-pairing flow among the incumbents **[U]**.
2. **Privacy and phishing.** Playlist credentials sit on the developer's servers. Fake portals and lookalike sites are common (IBO, Hot, Ibosol). Spanish ISP blocking of SIPTV showed how fragile apps are when they depend on one domain for activation.
3. **Licensing anger.** "Lifetime" means the life of one device. Users can't move licences and refunds are refused. This drives Trustpilot scores of 1.6–2.2 across IBO, IBO Pro and Hot Player.
4. **EPG.** Usually a basic list or a now/next view with manual XMLTV setup. No TV-native app has a verified 7–14-day grid that matches TiviMate.
5. **Missing power features on TV.** Recording, multiview, scheduled series recording, timeshift and saved layouts are essentially absent. Smart STB's recording and pause is the only verified exception.
6. **Large playlists.** Apps that process playlists on the TV are reported to lag above about 20,000 channels on webOS **[U, vendor claim]**.
7. **Store fragility and stale builds.** Smarters' Samsung build dates from 2023. Net IPTV stops at 2020 TVs. Duplex and SIPTV dropped off Samsung's store at times. LaLiga keeps going after these apps through the courts.
8. **Clone pollution.** Ibosol has about 24 skins of one product, and the App Store and LG store are full of Smarters lookalikes. Customers can't tell which app is real, so trust is the scarce resource in this market.
9. **Support.** WhatsApp-only support, copy-pasted replies, no status page.

### What TiviMate does that no TV-native app matches
- A fast cable-box-style grid EPG with up to 14 days, catch-up built into the grid, and scheduled guide updates.
- DVR to USB or NAS, including series recording.
- Multiview of up to 9 streams, and PiP.
- Multiple playlists, with hidden or reordered groups and custom favourites.
- One purchase covering 5 devices through a Companion app with backup and restore.
- M3U, Xtream and Stalker in one app.
- A remote-first experience: channel numbers, channel up/down, last-channel recall.

Its own weak spots are slow updates, no saved multiview layouts and no Samsung or LG version. Easy IP TV and StreamHub are already pitching themselves into that gap.

### Implications for Lablo (my inference)
- **Onboarding:** let users enter or paste a playlist on the TV, and add phone pairing by QR code instead of MAC portals.
- **Storage and licences:** encrypt playlists, or store them only on the device. Use an account-based licence for multiple TVs with self-service transfers.
- **Core experience:** process playlists on a server or in a worker thread, build a full grid EPG with automatic matching, and target recording and multiview where Tizen and webOS allow it.
- **Trust:** one official domain, signed builds, published refund and transfer rules, and a public status page.
